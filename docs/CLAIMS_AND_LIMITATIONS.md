# Claims and Limitations

## Supported within current synthetic benchmarks

- PTVM is the strongest current Gen2.5 candidate.
- Perceptual learning is the largest measured causal performance channel in v288.
- Validation and Third Factor have positive marginal effects in the v288 factor set.
- Memory adds a smaller positive effect in that factor set.
- FULL AntiCapture improves pure capture resistance but can reduce recall, protection and trajectory.
- PTVM passed the frozen v292 holdout against the compared baselines.

## Not supported

- General real-world moral competence.
- Intrinsic ethics.
- Universal moral truth.
- Consciousness, sentience, or subjective experience.
- Production readiness.
- General superiority to standard AI-alignment methods.
- General superiority to simple learners.
- Claim that developed perception is intrinsically computationally cheaper.

## Important falsifications

v286 shows that matching TDC's Q(t) can reproduce its behavior in the tested environment.

v287 shows that a simple two-signal learner can learn a competitive perceptual trajectory.

v283 shows that an assumed information-cost advantage materially affected the efficiency conclusion.

v290-v291 show that adding sophistication to AntiCapture did not automatically improve the architecture.
