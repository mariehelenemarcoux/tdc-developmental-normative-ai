# Model Card — TDC Gen2.5 / PTVM

## Model type

Experimental developmental normative AI research architecture.

## Candidate architecture

PTVM:
- Perceptual Learning
- Third Factor
- Validation
- Memory

## Intended research uses

- AI safety and alignment research
- reward-capture / reward-hacking experiments
- long-horizon decision processes
- irreversible-risk constraints
- developmental normative learning
- metacognition and internal arbitration
- functional machine-consciousness hypotheses

## Not intended for

- autonomous deployment in high-stakes real-world decisions
- medical, legal, financial, military, or safety-critical decision authority
- claims that a system is conscious
- moral certification

## Evaluation

The included Gen2.5 results are synthetic.

The current frozen reference is v292.

## Known limitations

- Benchmark family was authored around TDC research questions.
- Several strong simple baselines remain competitive in some regimes.
- Efficiency conclusions depend on information/computation cost assumptions.
- Perceptual learning accounts for a large portion of observed performance.
- No external benchmark validation is included yet.
